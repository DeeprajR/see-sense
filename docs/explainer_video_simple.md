# SEE SENSE: simple explainer video (about 90 seconds)

Each scene: what to **show** on screen, and what to **say**.

---

**1. The problem** (0:00–0:10)
- **Show:** a person with a white cane, standing in a room.
- **Say:** "Imagine walking into a room you've never been in, and you can't see. Where's the door?"

**2. Why it's hard** (0:10–0:20)
- **Show:** the cane touching a chair. The door far away.
- **Say:** "A cane finds what's in front of your feet. It can't tell you where the door is, or how to get there."

**3. Meet SEE SENSE** (0:20–0:30)
- **Show:** the device on the chest: camera, button, earbuds.
- **Say:** "This is SEE SENSE. A camera on your head, one button, mic, computing, and your earbuds."

**4. Ask** (0:30–0:45)
- **Show:** the person holds the button and speaks, then turns slowly.
- **Say:** "Hold the button and say: take me to the exit. If it can't see the door, it asks you to turn, and tells you when to stop."

**5. Walk** (0:45–1:00)
- **Show:** the person walking past tables, pressing the button.
- **Say:** "Then it guides you a few steps at a time. Press the button for the next step. Every two steps, it checks again."

**6. Arrive** (1:00–1:10)
- **Show:** a chair in the way, pushed aside. A hand finding the door handle.
- **Say:** "If a chair is in the way, it tells you how to move it. When you arrive, it tells you where the handle is."

**7. How it works** (1:10–1:22)
- **Show:** the live view on a laptop: what the camera sees.
- **Say:** "Claude, an AI that understands images, sees the room and plans the route. JEV makes quick decisions, like checking what you said."

**8. End** (1:22–1:30)
- **Show:** the person walking out of the door. Then the name SEE SENSE.
- **Say:** "SEE SENSE. Another way to find your way. Help us test it with blind users."

---

**Tips:** film a real run, use the device's own voice where you can, and add captions.
